## What is Cove?

Cove is a suite of products to boost your earnings, without the hassle. The
Cove ecosystem includes:

- **$COVE**: a currently non-transferable governance token.
- **Boosties**: the dedicated liquid locker and staking platform for Yearn,
  built with Yearn V3.
  - Choose between claiming
    [dYFI](https://docs.yearn.fi/getting-started/products/veyfi#dyfi) rewards
    directly or automatically compounding them.
  - **coveYFI**: a liquid locker for YFI.
  - **dYFI Converter**: a public good to automatically redeem dYFI rewards
    for YFI without holding ETH.
- **Cove Protocol**: the first onchain portfolio manager. Launching ~Q3 2024.
