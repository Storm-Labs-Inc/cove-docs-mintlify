Boosties is a liquid locker and staking platform that allows users to efficiently benefit from Yearn v3 **dYFI** emissions on their gauge tokens.

### Problem

Yearn v3 requires users to provide both **veYFI** (vote escrowed **YFI**) and gauge tokens in proportion to earn maximum boosted **dYFI** yield on their gauge tokens. Users that do not balance their positions suffer from earning the non-optimized **dYFI** emissions.

### Solution

Coves offers **Boosties**, a protocol that allows users to take full advantage of Yearn v3’s **dYFI** emissions through a protocol owned **veYFI** boost. This is done without requiring individuals to hold both **veYFI** and gauge tokens.

### Using Boosties

Depositing to a Boosties vault is easy. Any token related to the gauge including the vault token or underlying asset can be used to deposit.

1. Toggle if you would like to auto-compound rewards.
   1. Boosties is automatically configured to auto-compound rewards.
2. Select the respective vault for your tokens and click `Deposit`.
   1. Sign the approval to allow Boosties to use your tokens.
   2. Submit another transaction to deposit tokens.

The boost from protocol owned **veYFI** is immediately applied to users’ deposits. Users earn **COVE** tokens from their deposits as well.
