Boosties is a liquid locker and staking platform that allows users to efficiently benefit from Yearn's **dYFI** emissions on their gauge tokens.

### Problem

Yearn requires users to provide both **veYFI** (vote escrowed **YFI**) and gauge tokens in proportion to earn maximum boosted **dYFI** yield on their gauge tokens. Users that do not balance their positions suffer from earning the non-optimized **dYFI** emissions.

### Solution

**Boosties** allows users to take full advantage of Yearn’s **dYFI** emissions through a protocol owned **veYFI** boost. This is done without requiring individuals to hold both **veYFI** and gauge tokens.

### Using Boosties

Depositing to a Boosties vault is easy. Any token related to the gauge including the vault token or underlying asset can be used to deposit.

<Note>
  Boosties allows users to choose to auto-compound rewards or claim dYFI
  directly.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Deposit">
    Select the respective vault for your tokens and click `Deposit`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign Token Approval">
    Sign the approval to allow Boosties to use your tokens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit Transaction">
    Submit a final transaction to deposit tokens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The boost from protocol owned **veYFI** is immediately applied to users’ deposits. Users earn **COVE** tokens from their deposits as well.
