## Bazaar Launchpad

Bazaar is a decentralized, chain-agnostic launchpad providing crypto-natives
with a fair, community-centric approach to fundraising.

An [uncapped batch
auction](https://docs.bazaar.money/launch/launchpad-mechanics/batch-auctions-capped-and-uncapped)
will be held on [Bazaar](https://bazaar.money/) for 9.5% of the $COVE supply in
exchange for ETH to help bootstrap protocol owned liquidity.  The auction is
scheduled for April 22nd, 2024!

Funds will be used to buy up to 285 YFI (~\$2.4MM). ~\$300K of the purchased
YFI will be used to seed an initial coveYFI/YFI Curve pool as protocol owned
liquidity (PoL).  The purchase will be facilitated by the [community
multisig](/ecosystem/token/governance#community-multisig).

Excess funds will be held by the community multisig to be utilized by the DAO
through governance on [Snapshot](https://vote.cove.finance/#/).

<Note>
  At the moment, $COVE is non-transferable and only used for voting in protocol
  governance. In the future, we expect governance proposals to enable transfers
  and add additional utility and tokenomics.
</Note>

For more details on $COVE, please refer to the [token documentation](/ecosystem/token/info).

| % of token supply sold | 9.5%                   |
