## Deposits and Withdrawals

coveUSD offers flexible options for entering and exiting positions.

### Deposits

<Steps>
    <Step title="Deposit USDC">
        You can deposit USDC into the protocol at any time.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Asynchronous Processing">
        Your deposit is processed during the next rebalancing cycle.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Claim coveUSD">
        Your coveUSD tokens become available to claim after the rebalancing event completes.
        <Info>
          **Automated Claiming:** This step is handled automatically for you by our non-custodial off-chain bots. You can also claim your tokens manually if you prefer.
        </Info>
    </Step>
</Steps>

### Withdrawals

You have two options for withdrawing your funds from the vault.

<Tabs>
<Tab title="Pro-Rata Withdrawal">
    <Steps>
        <Step title="Redeem coveUSD">
            Redeem your `coveUSD` for a proportional share of the vault's underlying assets. This option is available at any time, except during rebalancing windows.
        </Step>
        <Step title="Receive Asset Basket">
            You will instantly receive a basket of the vault's current holdings (e.g., sUSDe, sfrxUSD, etc.).
        </Step>
    </Steps>
</Tab>
<Tab title="Single-Asset Withdrawal">
    <Steps>
        <Step title="Request USDC Withdrawal">
            You can request to withdraw your entire position in USDC.
        </Step>
        <Step title="Asynchronous Processing">
            The request is processed during the subsequent rebalancing event, which typically occurs within 24 hours.
        </Step>
        <Step title="Claim USDC">
            Your USDC becomes claimable after the rebalancing is complete.
            <Info>
              **Automated Claiming:** This step is handled automatically for you by our non-custodial off-chain bots. You can also claim your tokens manually if you prefer.
            </Info>
        </Step>
    </Steps>
</Tab>
</Tabs>
