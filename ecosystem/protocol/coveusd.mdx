## Overview

coveUSD is Cove's flagship vault that delivers institutional-grade USD yield through automated capital allocation. By pairing off-chain intelligence with on-chain automation, coveUSD seeks to establish the benchmark rate for safe, blue-chip USD yield on Ethereum.

### Key Features

- **Automated Optimization**: Routes deposits through off-chain Silverback bots and Gauntlet's institutional-grade risk models
- **Dynamic Reallocation**: Automatically reallocates capital across top-tier strategies to maximize risk-adjusted returns
- **Proven Performance**: Achieved net APYs up to 13.34% over the past three months
- **Transparent Governance**: Future active management secured behind a timelock for transparent governance

## How It Works

### Strategy Selection

coveUSD dynamically allocates capital across a curated selection of blue-chip USD yield strategies:

- **Ethena's sUSDe**: Synthetic dollar protocol offering sustainable yield
- **Frax's sfrxUSD**: Frax's staked USD stablecoin
- **Superform's SuperUSDC**: Multi-protocol yield optimization (Euler/Fluid)
- **Cove's Boosties sUSDS**: Maker/Spark yield vault

### Intelligent Rebalancing

The vault employs sophisticated off-chain intelligence to optimize allocations:

1. **Risk Analysis**: Gauntlet's models continuously assess risk/reward profiles across strategies
2. **Opportunity Detection**: Silverback bots monitor yield opportunities 24/7
3. **Efficient Execution**: Rebalancing occurs through Programmatic Orders to minimize costs
4. **Value Preservation**: Internal trades matched off-chain to avoid slippage and MEV

## Deposits and Withdrawals

coveUSD offers flexible options for entering and exiting positions:

### Deposits
- Deposit USDC into the protocol
- Deposits are processed asynchronously during rebalancing cycles
- Claim your coveUSD tokens after the next rebalancing event

### Withdrawals

**Option 1: Synchronous Pro-Rata Redemption**
- Redeem coveUSD for a proportional share of underlying assets
- Available at any time (except during rebalancing windows)
- Receive a basket of the vault's current holdings

**Option 2: Asynchronous USDC Withdrawal**
- Request to withdraw USDC directly
- Processed during the subsequent rebalancing event
- USDC becomes claimable after ~24 hours
