## Who is Storm Labs?

[Storm Labs](https://storm-labs.xyz/) is a software development studio that
builds infrastructure for the next financial generation. The Storm Labs team
is developing the initial version of the Cove Protocol. The company recently
announced a [$3M pre-seed fundraising
round](https://twitter.com/cove_fi/status/1777723825288831384) led by [Electric
Capital](https://electriccapital.com/), with participation from
[Accomplice](https://accomplice.co/), [Robot Ventures](https://robvc.com/),
[Daedalus](https://www.daedalus.gg/), and other notable angel investors:

### Team

The team is comprised of DeFi veterans, led by
[devops199fan](https://twitter.com/devops199fan), an early YFI contributor,
founding partner at [eGirl Capital](https://www.egirlcapital.com/), and previous
founder of Saddle, the premier open-source StableSwap with $2.2B+ in volume.
