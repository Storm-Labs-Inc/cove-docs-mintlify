## Who is Ritual?

[Ritual](https://ritual.net/) is an open, sovereign execution layer for AI.
Seamlessly integrate AI into your app or protocol on any chain, enabling you to
fine-tune, monetize, and perform inference on models with cryptographic
schemes.

With Ritual, the vision is for developers to be able to build fully transparent
DeFi, self-improving blockchains, autonomous agents, generated content, and
more.

### How is Ritual involved with Cove?

Cove is collaborating with Ritual on AI-enabled strategies. Stay tuned for more
updates soon!
