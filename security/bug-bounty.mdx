[Bug bounty programs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bug_bounty_program) pay
security researchers ("white hats") to find and responsibly disclose
vulnerabilities before attackers can exploit them. Cove hosts its bounty on
[Immunefi](https://immunefi.com/), the leading platform for smart-contract
security.

<Note>
  All submissions must follow Immunefi's Responsible Disclosure Policy.
</Note>
