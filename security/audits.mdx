Smart contract audits are critical for identifying and fixing security
vulnerabilities before deployment. They help prevent financial losses and
maintain trust in blockchain protocols by ensuring the code is robust and
error-free.

Cove has been audited by [Trail of Bits](https://trailofbits.com/),
[Quantstamp](https://quantstamp.com/), and [yAudit](https://yaudit.dev/).

<Note>
  It is important to remember that audits are not a security guarantee.
</Note>
