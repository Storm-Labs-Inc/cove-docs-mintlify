## Using Boosties

### Depositing

Depositing to a Boosties vault is easy. First, you'll need to deposit into the
Yearn vault linked at the top of the description and then stake in [the
corresponding gauge](https://veyfi.yearn.fi/).

<Note>
  Make sure you have deposited into the Yearn vault **and** staked in the gauge
  before depositing on Boosties.  In the future, any token related to the gauge
  including the vault token or underlying asset can be used to deposit.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Deposit">
    Select the respective vault for your tokens and click `Deposit`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign Token Approval">
    Sign the approval to allow Boosties to use your tokens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit Transaction">
    Submit a final transaction to deposit tokens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The boost from protocol owned **veYFI** is immediately applied to users’
deposits. Users earn **COVE** tokens from their deposits as well once liquidity
mining begins.

### Withdrawing

Withdrawing your deposits is a breeze. Sign an approval message
(gasless transaction) and then submit a withdrawal transaction.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Withdraw">
    Select the respective vault for your tokens and click `Withdraw`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign Token Approval">
    Sign the approval to allow Boosties to use your Cove LP tokens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit Transaction">
    Submit a final transaction to withdraw Yearn LP tokens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Upon withdrawal you will receive the Yearn vault token.
